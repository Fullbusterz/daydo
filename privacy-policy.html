Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

Your privacy matters to us. DayDo is a simple task management app designed to help you organize your day. This policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

DayDo stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

The app stores the following data locally:

  • Tasks you create (title, description, due date, priority, category)
  • App preferences and settings (language, theme)

2. How We Use Your Information

All data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app — managing your tasks. Your data never leaves your device and is not shared with any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your tasks and preferences are stored using Android's local storage (AsyncStorage). This data is private to the app and is deleted when you uninstall DayDo.

4. Third-Party Services

DayDo does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any similar platforms.

5. Permissions

DayDo may request the following device permissions:

  • Notifications — to remind you of upcoming tasks (optional, you can disable this at any time)

No other permissions are requested or required.

6. Children's Privacy

DayDo does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no age-restricted content.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
